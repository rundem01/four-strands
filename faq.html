Questions

Worth knowing before the first row.

What is Four Strands?

A quiet ledger for daily living. Each day you mark four things — Scripture, dumbbells, food, and sleep — and every day becomes a row woven into a record you can watch hold or fray.

What are the four strands?
  • Scripture — a passage read and sat with.
  • The iron — dumbbells, or honest recovery.
  • Food — one focus kept through the day.
  • Sleep — hours held, screens put down.

Mark all four in a day and that row is solid; miss one and it shows as a gap.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and nowhere else. No accounts, no server — your marks live in your browser's storage. The privacy policy has the full picture.

How do I back up my record, or move to a new device?

Export downloads your record as a small file; keep it somewhere safe and import it on any device to restore. Do this before clearing your browser or switching phones — local storage doesn't survive either on its own.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit it caches itself and runs fully offline — marks, the weave, and every guide.

How do I install it like an app?

On iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: open in Chrome and tap Install, or Add to Home screen from the menu. It then opens full-screen from its own icon.

Which Bible translations does it carry?

The Berean Standard Bible by default, plus the World English Bible and the King James Version. All are public domain or CC0 and live inside the app, which is part of why it needs no internet.

What happens if I miss a day?

The weave shows a gap for that day. Nothing is deleted and nothing resets — the record is honest but patient. The point is the next row, not a spotless run.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Four Strands is free, with no ads and no locked features.
