Privacy

Your record stays with you.

Effective 26 August 2026

The short version: everything you log lives on this device. No accounts, no server, no analytics, no ads. Your record never reaches us — we couldn't read it if we tried.

What it keeps

A small amount of data, held in your browser's storage on your device only:

  • Your daily marks for the four strands — Scripture, dumbbells, food, and sleep — by date.
  • Your preferences, such as light or dark theme.

That's the whole list. No sign-up, and no name, email, location, or contacts collected.

What it doesn't do

  • No accounts and no passwords.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising or third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing — there is nothing to sell or share.

Backups you make

Export saves your record as a file on your device. Where it lives, and whether you carry it to another device, is your call. Import only ever moves your own data between your own devices.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by Vercel, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses when a page loads; that is covered by Vercel's privacy policy. Four Strands adds nothing on top.

Deleting everything

Because it's all local, you're one step from a clean slate: clear this site's data in your browser, or remove the installed app. That erases your record from the device for good — so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Changes

If Four Strands ever changes how it handles data — say, an optional sync one day — this page updates first, with a new date, before anything takes effect.

Questions

Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository, or use the contact details on the home page.
